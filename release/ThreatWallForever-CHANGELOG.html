🇬🇧 English

Version 0.6 (06.11.2026)

✨ New

  • Classic view for target and focus – instead of the wall, optionally a window like the list: your group listed below each other, whoever holds the enemy on top
  • Debuff bar – your effects on the enemy, e.g. Sunder Armor, seals or curses, right on the TWF nameplates (feature request by Toca)
  • Reset everything – resets TWF completely if you want, like a fresh install

🔧 Changed

  • Starting position – list, target and focus sit neatly side by side on a new install
  • Settings – consistently Enable list / target / focus, matching options line up across all columns

🐞 Fixed

  • Checkboxes in the settings – an edge was sometimes missing at a small UI scale

Version 0.5.2 (05.11.2026)

🔧 Changed

  • Defaults for new installs – the list shows right away, even solo; target wall on, focus wall off; as a tank the list shows your lead

🐞 Fixed

  • Wall frame – was sometimes distorted after starting the game

Version 0.5.1 (19.10.2026)

🐞 Fixed

  • Focus and target wall – now sit at the right screen edge if your side bars are elsewhere or hidden; before, they could disappear

Version 0.5 (19.10.2026)

✨ New

  • Focus and target wall – who holds the mob, how close your group is to aggro, plus its raid marker
  • Raid markers – in the list and the walls, even in combat
  • Preview bar – shows right away in the Nameplates tab how a state looks
  • Health color – new table row for the health text
  • New options – target frame color, show list, walls only in combat or in a group, opacity
  • Version notice – tells you in chat when someone in your group or guild has a newer version
  • What's new? – this window (/twf news)

🔧 Changed

  • List – bars in the state color with health, marker, aggro % and aggro target
  • Settings – three tabs: General, Nameplates, View
  • Renamed – “List all enemies”, “Lock everything”, “Opacity”
  • Name – now written “Threat Wall Forever” in game

🐞 Fixed

  • “Locked” hint – now names the right way to unlock
  • Role sync tooltip – a character was shown as a box

Version 0.4 (18.10.2026)

✨ New

  • Tank lead – lead over 2nd place instead of aggro %, separately for bar and list
  • Health as a number – remaining health instead of percent
  • Role sync – TWF follows your group role

🔧 Changed

  • Minimap button – right-click opens the settings
  • List – room for four-digit percentages

Version 0.3 (17.10.2026)

✨ New

  • Español and Français – new languages, translations can be improved on GitHub
  • Color table – color, opacity, blinking and bar for every state, optionally per tank/DPS/heal
  • Aggro % in the list – live, in the state color
  • Heal role – tank, DPS and heal separate

🔧 Changed

  • Settings – with tabs
  • Window height – grows with the number of enemies (min/max rows)

🐞 Fixed

  • List – enemies appeared twice in instances

Version 0.2 (17.10.2026)

✨ New

  • Aggro in % – on the left of the nameplate bar
  • Blinking frame – on aggro problems
  • Second tank – brown instead of red, no false alarm
  • Foreign enemies – dark grey, not in the list
  • Opacity – sliders for target and behind walls

Version 0.1 (13.10.2026)

✨ New

  • Aggro list – only enemies with a problem, in traffic-light colors, for tank and DPS/heal
  • TWF nameplates – bars in aggro colors with health
  • Aggro target – role icon of whoever has aggro, at the enemy
