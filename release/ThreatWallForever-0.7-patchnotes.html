🇬🇧 English

Warnings your way, a reminder for paladin tanks and a debuff bar that goes where you want it.

✨ New

  • 🔔 Warning sound of your choice: eight sounds to browse and preview right in the settings, played through your effects or master volume.
  • 🟥 Screen edge: the edge of your screen flashes briefly in a color of your choice when things get dangerous – together with the warning sound. Opacity adjustable, with a live preview.
  • 👥 Warnings only in a group: solo it stays quiet.
  • 🏰 Only in instances: list, target and focus can be limited to dungeons and raids.
  • 🛡️ Paladin tanks: a reminder before Righteous Fury runs out – a message in the middle of the screen with sound at 20 and 10 minutes, then every minute from 5, and when it is missing. New section Class abilities in the General tab.
  • 🎯 Debuff bar: now above or below the bar and in the size you like, shown live in the preview. (player request)

🔧 Changed

  • 🏷️ Window titles: the list is now called Threat Wall Forever; target and focus show “Players on target” and “Players on focus”.
  • 🔊 Warning sound: now plays through the effects volume by default – switch to master if you prefer.

🐞 Fixed

  • 🤝 Second tank: no more warning sound when another tank holds the enemy.
