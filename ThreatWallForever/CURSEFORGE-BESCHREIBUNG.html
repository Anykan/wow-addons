🇬🇧 The threat meter, reinvented – see problems instead of reading numbers.

Classic threat meters give you long tables and leave it to you to find out where things are going wrong. Threat Wall Forever takes a new approach: it shows you only what matters right now – exactly where you are already looking. One glance, and you know where to step in.

You decide how: right on every enemy, in a compact list or on a wall for your whole group – one of them or all together. For tanks, DPS and healers.

  • 🎯 Aggro on every enemy – not just your target.
  • 🧱 Your whole group at a glance – who holds the mob, who is about to pull?
  • 💀 Raid markers – even in the middle of combat.
  • 🛡️ Made for tanks – keep your lead in sight, a second tank without false alarms.
  • 💚 Great for healers – see aggro problems without targeting a single enemy (enemy nameplates on).
  • 🎨 Your style – what is shown, where and in which colors.

Innovative, clear, built for WoW Forever. Read less, react faster.

Innovant, clair, conçu pour WoW Forever. Moins lire, réagir plus vite.
